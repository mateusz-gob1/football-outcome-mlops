For context: comparing to the betting market

The bookmaker's own odds (converted to probabilities) are a famously hard-to-beat baseline. This isn't the project's goal. It's here as an honest point of reference, using a bootstrap 95% CI on (model log-loss − bookmaker log-loss), 2000 resamples.

ModelDiff95% CI low95% CI high

Positive numbers mean the model's log-loss is higher (worse) than the bookmaker's. None of the three models close that gap, which is expected, since none of them use bookmaker odds as an input (see the project's README for why).
